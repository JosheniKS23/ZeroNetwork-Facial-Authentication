Datalake 3.0 Integration Hooks

beforeSensitiveAction()Require recent liveness token before opening protected records.
offlineAccessDecision()Return allow, step-up, deny, or fallback based on local policy.
appendSignedAudit()Write tamper-evident event chain for later synchronization.
rotateLocalKeys()Re-wrap local vault material after recovery or admin unlock.
rejectAmbiguousCapture()Reject zero-face and multi-face frames before any protected workflow continues.
queueOfflineAttendance()Store signed attendance packets locally and sync only after approved connectivity returns.
